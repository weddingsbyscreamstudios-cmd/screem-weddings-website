# Website images and videos: where to put them

**The repository is public.** Everything in this folder can be seen and downloaded by anyone on the internet.

## Before adding any file
1. **The couple has agreed** to their photos/film being on the website (consent recorded in their wedding `client/` folder, open decision D-17).
2. **The music is cleared** in the licence register (for videos).
3. **It is an export, never an original.** Never put files from a wedding's `originals/` here.

## Folders
| Folder | What goes in it | Size to export |
|---|---|---|
| `images/hero/` | 1 main photo for the top of the home page | 2400 px wide, JPG or WebP, under 500 KB |
| `images/films/` | 1 cover still per film card | 1600 × 900 px (16:9), under 300 KB each |
| `images/about/` | Photo of you / the team | 1200 × 1500 px (4:5), under 300 KB |
| `images/gallery/` | Extra photos | 1600 px on the long side, under 300 KB each |
| `videos/hero/` | Short silent background loop (showreel) | 10–20 s, 1920 × 1080, MP4 (H.264), no sound, under 10 MB |
| `videos/films/` | Short preview clips only | MP4 (H.264), under 25 MB each |

**Full films do not go here.** GitHub refuses files over 100 MB and slows down with large videos. Upload full films to YouTube or Vimeo (unlisted or public, with the couple's consent) and the page will embed them.

## File names
Lower case, hyphens, no spaces or couple names, e.g. `hero-01.jpg`, `film-01-cover.jpg`, `showreel-loop.mp4`.

After adding files, tell Claude which file goes where and it will connect them to the page.
